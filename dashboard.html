How to read this

Model's chance is the forecast's probability that the temperature lands in that range. Market's price is what Kalshi traders are paying, in cents per $1. A contract at 30¢ means the market thinks there's about a 30% chance.

A signal appears when the model's chance beats the price by at least 6¢ after fees. BUY YES bets the temperature lands in that range; BUY NO bets it doesn't.

Is the model honest? compares how often trades actually won with how often the model expected them to. If those drift far apart over 100+ trades, the model needs tuning, whatever the profit says.
